# Wallpaper, your way — photos, patterns, and your own image

Settings → Background now lets you set the backdrop for the whole app:

- **8 bundled photos** and **6 subtle patterns** (dots, blueprint, diagonal,
  accent dots, scanlines, grid).
- **Your image**: upload your own photo — it's downscaled locally, stored on
  this device only (never synced, never uploaded anywhere), and can be swapped
  or deleted any time via the × on its thumbnail.
- Two **Frost sliders** (Frost & Terminal). *Frost* controls the hero-card
  blur; *Terminal* makes the terminal backdrop translucent so the backdrop
  bleeds through — and the note editor follows the same treatment now.
- Sidebar, home card and every Settings section sit on the same frosted layers,
  so everything reads as one calm surface.
