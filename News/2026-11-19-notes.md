# Notes, synced with everything else

RuTerm now has **free-form Markdown notes**, stored in the same vault as your
hosts and identities. Credentials are sealed field-by-field; note text stays
readable JSON locally and in your own Drive sync, while self-hosted sync seals
the whole payload.

- Sidebar → Notes: create, search, favorite, delete. The list titles come from
  the first words of the note.
- Click a note to open it as a **full tab** — its own pane, its own position in
  the tab bar, auto-saved as you type.
- Notes **survive a reload** (⌘R) together with their tabs — and unsaved edits
  are flushed when the window is about to close, so a fast reload won't eat
  what you just wrote.
- An empty note opens straight in edit mode; the rendered view is a single
  click away. Raw Markdown, GFM flavor (tables, task lists, strikethrough).
