# Sync that tells you the truth

Your vault's sync status is now honest and visible:

- **Green check** — this device matches the cloud exactly.
- **Red X** — something changed locally that hasn't been pushed yet (or you're
  offline).
- **"Last synced … ago"** — relative time of the last successful sync,
  persisted across restarts, updated by Sync now, background auto-push, or a
  new device adopting the vault.
- **Google sign-in got a real identity**: your Google profile photo, display
  name and Google account ID now show up in Settings → Google and sync, and
  the sidebar footer shows your photo + name instead of the raw email.
- No more revision numbers on the screen — and the internal `revision 32`
  readout is gone for good.
- The sync bridge (v1.x, redeployed) now stores and returns your Google avatar,
  so the photo persists across sign-out/sign-in cycles.
