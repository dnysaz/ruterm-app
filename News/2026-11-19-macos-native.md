# A native macOS look — flat, quiet, two themes

The whole interface got a redesign: **two themes** (Dark and Light) in place of
the three ember variants, flat surfaces with hairline separators, and **no
shadows** anywhere. Grouped inset lists, macOS-style toggles, sliders and
segmented controls replace the old chrome — it finally *feels* like a Mac app.

- **Sidebar** is translucent with a backdrop blur, so your desktop (or the
  backdrop you picked in Settings) shows through.
- **Settings** was rebuilt from scratch: even spacing, clearer section titles,
  consistent switches and sliders, tidier thumbnails.
- **Native right-click is off** everywhere except real text fields (the terminal
  and the app draw their own menus); external links open in your browser behind
  a confirmation dialog.
- Panel chrome got quieter: the **Home tab is pinned** (always first, never
  closable) and About/Terms/Privacy/Contact/GitHub are one click away on the
  home screen.
