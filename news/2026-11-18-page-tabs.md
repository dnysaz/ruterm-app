# Settings & host editor became full tabs

No more slide-over sheets. **Settings** and the **host editor** now open as regular
tabs in the tab bar — same reach as SSH sessions, with their own split panes, drag
reordering and right-click menu.

- Sidebar gear opens the Settings tab; double-click a host (or its ellipsis menu →
  *Edit host*) opens the host editor tab.
- Both are singletons: re-opening them focuses the existing tab instead of stacking.
- Page tabs never survive a restart, and they're hidden from session-only menu items
  (Split right/down, Duplicate).

Also new: **sessions persist across Cmd+R** — tabs and their scrollback are saved
locally and replayed on reload, with SSH reconnecting below the restored text.
Local-only scrollback, never synced; toggle it in Settings → Sessions.
